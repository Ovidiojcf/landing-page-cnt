## Users

People visiting the company website to learn about ferry crossings, including passengers and people transporting vehicles. No single audience profile is confirmed as the priority.

## Product Purpose

Camila Navegação e Transportes provides ferry transportation for passengers and vehicles between cities. The website presents its services and company information so visitors can understand the available routes and fleet.

## Positioning

The website presents passenger and vehicle ferry crossings on the routes operated by Camila Navegação e Transportes. No unique differentiator over other operators has been confirmed.

- It presents the company's fleet, awards, and ferry routes.
- The destination and behavior for future schedules and fares information are undecided; do not invent a URL or promise a schedule lookup.
