## AMA Project – Support for Teen Mothers

Teenage pregnancy is one of the social challenges faced by many communities in northern Brazil. In many cases, young women go through this experience without the preparation and support they need to care for themselves and their babies.

In Cametá, Pará, where Camila Navegação operates one of its branches, this is also a significant social issue. In response, Camila Navegação partnered with ADRA Brazil to create the **AMA Project (Support for Teen Mothers)**, an initiative dedicated to welcoming, guiding, and supporting pregnant teenagers and young mothers.

Studies conducted by the project found that approximately **30% of pregnant women in the municipality of Cametá are between 13 and 19 years old**. This means that around 30 out of every 100 pregnant women are teenagers.

The main goal of the AMA Project is to provide guidance and support to pregnant teenagers aged 13 to 19, as well as their mothers and partners, helping them experience healthier pregnancies and provide better care for their babies. This support includes prenatal guidance, maternal healthcare throughout pregnancy, and information on essential newborn care.

In addition to supporting young women throughout pregnancy and providing guidance to their families, the project focuses on two other important goals:

- **Keeping girls in school:** encouraging participants to continue their education and helping prevent school dropout during and after pregnancy.
- **Building professional skills:** offering career workshops and income-generating activities to prepare participants for future employment opportunities.

Since many participants come from vulnerable backgrounds, the project also provides essential supplies, including baby layettes, diapers, maternity clothes, educational materials, and food for newborns.
