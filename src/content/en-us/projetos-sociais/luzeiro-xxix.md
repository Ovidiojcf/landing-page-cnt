## Luzeiro XXIX Project

The first Luzeiro boat was inaugurated in July 1931 by Leo and Jessie Halliwell, with the goal of bringing health education and free medical and dental care to riverside communities in northern Brazil. Thousands of people benefited directly from the project. In many cases, it was the only way they could receive medical or dental care.

After more than 30 years away from the state of Pará, the Luzeiro Project returned with the Luzeiro XXIX boat through a partnership between the ADRA Humanitarian Agency and Camila Navegação, which donated the vessel. The project's main purpose is to bring healthcare to riverside communities through medical and dental care, as well as other health specialties.

To learn more about the Luzeiro Project in Pará and ADRA's other humanitarian projects, visit [www.adra.org.br](https://www.adra.org.br/).
