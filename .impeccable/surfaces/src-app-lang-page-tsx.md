# Scope

Homepage Services section. Mode: Persuade.

# Visitor and content

Visitors need to scan the ferry routes available for passengers and vehicles. Present the six supplied origin/destination pairs and their states. No service schedule, fare, or destination for the schedules label is available.

# Direction contract

THESIS: Make route coverage easy to scan; refuse map imagery and grouped state panels.

OWN-WORLD: Extend the existing typography with a full-width brand-red field and compact warm cream route cards. Use the project's established fonts, dark navy text, and restrained elevation.

STORY: Visitors recognize the service, compare each origin with its destination, and see the state without inferring schedules or booking.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Center the title and explanatory line above one responsive grid: three columns on desktop and at least two on tablet and mobile. Each compact card pairs state, origin, a continuously moving dashed route, destination, and a quiet schedules label.

FORM: A direct local extension of the established homepage, specified by the user; no concept tournament applies. Memorable moment: the route dash travels when a card is hovered. Seed: none (not applicable; local extension).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

# Constraints and unresolved decisions

Use no icons or maps, and no eyebrow above the title. Keep route cards compact. The schedules and fares label remains visual-only until its destination and behavior are confirmed.
